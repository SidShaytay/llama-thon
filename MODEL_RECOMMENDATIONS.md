## Quantization Quality Analysis

### Impact on Reasoning Performance

| Quantization | Quality Loss | Memory Reduction | Recommendation |
|--------------|--------------|------------------|----------------|
| **Q8_0** | Minimal (~1-2%) | 50% vs FP16 | Production-critical tasks |
| **Q6_K** | Very low (~2-5%) | 62.5% vs FP16 | "Almost lossless", excellent default |
| **Q5_K_M** | Low (~5-10%) | 68.75% vs FP16 | Good middle ground |
| **Q4_K_M** | Moderate (~10-15%) | 75% vs FP16 | Widely useful, maintains 95% quality |
| **Q3_K_M** | High (~20-30%) | 81.25% vs FP16 | Significant degradation |

### Critical Findings from Research

**Q6_K Quantization:**
- Provides "almost lossless" behavior, nearly indistinguishable from original
- Quality remains in "acceptable for most uses" range
- Most users find Q6 sufficient for actual work

**Q4_K_M Quantization:**
- K-quant formats preserve salient weights better → more stable reasoning, fewer derails
- Uses 3.80GB instead of 13GB while maintaining 95% quality
- Often as accurate as the original model for practical tasks

**Q3 Quantization:**
- Shows +0.2437 perplexity increase at 7B parameters with "very high quality loss"
- At 7B+, creativity, quality output, and nuance drop significantly
- Instruction following can be inconsistent
- **Critical for reasoning:** Abstract mathematical reasoning density appears insufficient

**Model Size Impact:**
- For 13B+ models, Q4 vs Q6 difference in nuance and instruction following can be "huge"
- Larger models (70B+) at Q4 often outperform smaller models (13B-30B) at Q6

*Sources:* [Demystifying Quantization Suffixes](https://medium.com/@paul.ilvez/demystifying-llm-quantization-suffixes-what-q4-k-m-q8-0-and-q6-k-really-mean-0ec2770f17d3), [Practical Quantization Guide](https://enclaveai.app/blog/2025/11/12/practical-quantization-guide-iphone-mac-gguf/), [GGUF Quantizations Overview](https://gist.github.com/Artefact2/b5f810600771265fc1e39442288e8ec9), [AI Model Quantization 2025](https://local-ai-zone.github.io/guides/what-is-ai-quantization-q4-k-m-q8-gguf-guide-2025.html)
